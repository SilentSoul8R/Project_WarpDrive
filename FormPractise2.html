Release Date:

There's no official release date yet. As of the most recent update: the confirmed facts are PC focus, Skyrim: Special Edition engine, Morrowind foundation, and a volunteer team still building, the official press pack lists Skywind's release date as TBA, with no confirmed launch window. Progress-wise, as of the May 2026 update: 2D, 3D, visual effects, writing, and voice acting are roughly 80–99 percent complete, while sound design and voice mastering lag behind at 65 and 12 percent respectively. With 10 out of 13 regions nearing completion, the geography of Vvardenfell is largely represented in the Skyrim engine, while the remaining work on Red Mountain represents one of the most complex areas of the map. TheGamer In Game News Given it's a 14-year volunteer project (started 2012) that keeps sliding, I'd treat any "2026/2027" guess with real skepticism — Skyblivion is the more advanced sibling project and it just slipped from 2025 into 2026 for similar reasons (Imperial City assets, navmesh /quest work).

Requirements:

Players will need Skyrim: Special Edition and Morrowind with its expansions to play Skywind. Specifically:

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim
Special Edition (a legal copy, on Steam or elsewhere)
The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind
Game of the Year Edition (likely, per Skywind's own wiki description) which bundles Tribunal and Bloodmoon
Both installed and pointed at by the Skywind installer, since it repurposes Skyrim's engine/systems while pulling some source data from Morrowind
